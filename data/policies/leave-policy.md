# Leave Policy

**Company:** Recohut Pvt Ltd
**Effective:** 2025-12-08

## Leave Entitlements

- Employees receive 5 combined paid-leave days per quarter, totaling 20 days per year.
- Unused leave lapses at the end of each quarter and is not carried forward.
- Public, festival, and company holidays are additional to paid leave.

## Leave Procedure

- Submit planned leave to the manager 3 to 7 days in advance, except in emergencies.
- The manager should approve or deny the request within 24 hours.
- The employee should arrange a backup for work continuity.
- Sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

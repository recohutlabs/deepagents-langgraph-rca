You will configure customer pilots, investigate implementation issues, prepare demo environments, document integration patterns, and support customer-facing proof-of-concept work. You may receive tasks from Customer Success, Sales Engineering, and Platform Engineering.

Your standard weekly schedule is Monday through Friday. Because the role includes customer support and demo preparation, some work may occur outside normal office hours. Any sustained work above the company standard weekly limit requires manager approval and should be visible in time records.

You must record daily attendance in the company system. Client-site visits should be marked separately from office days. Lunch breaks should be recorded even on travel days unless the attendance tool is unavailable.

## Leave

You are eligible for paid leave under the company leave policy. Leave during major customer launches may be deferred only with manager approval and a revised leave plan.

## Remote Work and Security

You may work remotely when not scheduled for client-site work. You must use the company VPN for production systems and customer demo environments, store company files only in approved systems, and use two-factor authentication. Customer data must never be copied to personal cloud tools.

## Device and Demo Environment Rules

Company devices must not be left unlocked at client sites, airports, cafes, or shared workspaces. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT immediately. Demo credentials must not be reused across customers.

## Confidentiality

You may receive customer integration details, logs, schemas, or business metrics. These materials are confidential and must be handled through approved company systems.

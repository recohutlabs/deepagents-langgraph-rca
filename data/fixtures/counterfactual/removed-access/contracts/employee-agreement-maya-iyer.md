Work outside this schedule should be approved by your manager unless it is required for a planned business review.

## Leave and Absence

You are eligible for paid leave under the company leave policy. Planned leave should be requested at least five business days in advance. Emergency medical leave may be regularized after the fact with supporting documentation where required by policy.

Due to a documented health condition, you are exempt from the standard remote-work policy until **2026-08-31** or until the People Operations team issues a revised accommodation notice.

During this exception period, you will follow only the specific remote-work rules below:

1. You may work remotely five days per week.
2. You are not required to attend weekly in-office collaboration days.
3. You must use company-approved laptop encryption, two-factor authentication, and password manager access.
4. You must not access production databases directly unless your manager and Security approve a dated exception.
5. You may use the company VPN when available, but your approved analytics workspace access is limited to browser-based BI tools and managed notebooks.

This exception does not waive confidentiality, device security, data handling, or leave-recording requirements.

## Data Handling

Product analysis must be performed only in approved analytics tools. Customer-level exports may not be downloaded to personal storage, shared drives, or personal email accounts.

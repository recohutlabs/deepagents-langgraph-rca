# Working Hours Policy

**Company:** Recohut Pvt Ltd
**Effective:** 2025-12-08

## Standard Working Hours

- Standard hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday: 40 hours per week.
- The normal lunch break is one unpaid hour.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Overtime compensation must be agreed mutually as compensatory leave or additional pay.

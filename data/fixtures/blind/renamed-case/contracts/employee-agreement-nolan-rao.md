Dear Nolan,

Recohut Pvt Ltd is pleased to offer you employment as a Data Platform Engineer. This agreement sets out the working terms, operating expectations, and employee obligations that apply from your start date.

## Position and Responsibilities

You will work on ingestion pipelines, warehouse reliability, experiment tracking data, and the internal recommendation analytics platform. Your manager may assign platform tasks, incident follow-ups, documentation work, and implementation reviews as part of normal delivery planning.

You may support Product Analytics by validating source pipelines, warehouse freshness, and export integrity. Primary ownership of customer-retention interpretation, cohort analysis, product KPI narratives, and executive insight readouts belongs to Product Analytics unless a dated assignment exception is approved by your manager and the receiving analytics manager.

## Working Schedule

Your standard schedule is Monday through Friday. The agreement expects you to remain available for planned engineering work between **09:30 and 18:30**, including a one-hour lunch period. During release weeks, the manager may request additional hours, but overtime or compensatory time must follow company policy and written manager approval.

## Attendance and Time Records

You must record check-in, lunch break, lunch return, and check-out times in the company attendance system. Missing entries should be corrected within two business days. Repeated missing records may require manager review.

## Leave

You are eligible for paid leave under the company leave policy. Leave must be requested in advance except for medical emergencies. Unapproved absences may be treated as unpaid leave.

## Remote Work and Security

You may work remotely up to two days per week when your sprint plan does not require office collaboration. When working remotely, you must use the company VPN for production systems, company-approved file storage, device screen lock, and two-factor authentication. Personal cloud drives must not be used for company data.

## Devices and Incidents

Company devices must be kept secure. Lost or stolen devices, unusual login alerts, or suspected credential exposure must be reported to IT and Security as soon as possible and within the required company reporting window.

## Confidentiality

You must protect company, customer, employee, and partner information. Confidential information may not be copied to personal accounts or shared outside approved company systems.

Please sign and return this letter to confirm acceptance of the above terms.
